About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple notes app for catching an idea the moment you have it. You type a note, add it to your list, and see at a glance how many notes you have.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Go to the Home page and click the "New note" box.
  2. Type your idea, for example "Revise HTML forms".
  3. Press the "Add Note" button, or hit Enter.
  4. Check the "Your notes" list to see your note, and the count above it.

Features

  • Add a note in one step
  • See all your notes in a single list
  • A running count of how many notes you have
  • Works in any modern web browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you have typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
